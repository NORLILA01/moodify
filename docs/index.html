WELCOME TO MOODIFY

What Is Moodify?

A Smarter Way to Understand Emotions and Change the Way We Respond.

The name Moodify combines mood and modify. Students may not always control what they feel, but they can learn to understand their emotions and modify how they respond.

Moodify does not simply tell students what to say. It provides a safe, supervised space to practise, reflect and make their own decisions.

Unlike ChatGPT or a conventional chatbot, Moodify is not designed to provide general answers. It is a bounded, psychology-based AI learning environment that guides adolescents aged 13–15 through five steps:

Write→Detect→Pause & Identify→Compare→Evaluate & Revise
